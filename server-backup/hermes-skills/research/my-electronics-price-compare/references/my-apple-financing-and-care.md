# MY Apple financing + AppleCare+ (decision rules)

Re-verify live at checkout. Rules below are structural, not a live rate card.

## Apple official 0% instalment
- Source page pattern: `https://www.apple.com/my/shop/browse/financing`
- Typical: **0% for 6 / 12 / 24 months** via participating **Visa/Mastercard credit cards**
- Order minimum commonly **RM2,000** single transaction
- Available online **and** Apple Store (e.g. The Exchange TRX)
- **NOT eligible:** Education Store orders, EPP, business/institutional, non-CC payment
- **No SPayLater / Lazada PayLater** at Apple

## Education vs instalment trade-off
| Goal | Path |
|---|---|
| Lowest **device cash** + promo GC | `apple.com/my-edu` or TRX Education verify → full debit/FPX/cash |
| Official **0% long tenure** | Consumer purchase with CC at Apple/online (lose Edu sticker; may keep other promos — check) |
| No CC, need split pay | Marketplace Mall/Official + BNPL **or** borrow 0% cash to hit Edu full pay |

**Effective value example shape (always recompute with live numbers):**
- Edu sticker − gift-card face value = net if GC spent on Care/accessories user wants
- Mall voucher cash total
- Mall/BNPL: cash down + Σ instalments (include interest)

If BNPL interest ≳ gift-card or Edu delta, prefer delay / family 0% loan / shorter 0% BNPL.

## BNPL (Shopee/Lazada PayLater) — how to advise
1. Read **tenure table on screenshot**, not marketing “from RM…”.
2. Prefer platform **0% 1-month or 3-month** when user can cash-flow the monthly.
3. If user can only afford long tenure (12–24m) at **~1.25–1.5%/month**, show:
   - Approx flat interest ≈ `balance × rate × months`
   - Total paid vs Edu+GC cash
4. Partial pay: cash/coins first, minimise principal that bears interest.
5. Never claim Apple Store accepts SPayLater.

## AppleCare+ (Mac)
- Sold as **multi-year accidental-damage plan** (commonly **3 years** one-time on MY resellers).
- Live price: check Apple bag add-on **or** APP Shopify add-on product titled `AppleCare+ for … MacBook Air …`.
- Session-verified ballpark for **13" MacBook Air M5**: **RM929** one-time at Machines/Switch (re-fetch).
- Accidental service **excess** (from Apple MY buy footnotes; re-check): screen/enclosure often **RM469**; other accidental often **RM1,429**.
- Shop “free extra 1-year warranty” (e.g. senQ) is **not** AppleCare+ (usually no unlimited ADH).
- Marketplace **EPS/electronic protection** ≠ AppleCare+.
- Budget order: device → cheap video adapters → AppleCare+ if still needed (confirm remaining days to add Care after purchase).

## MacBook Air + desk monitors (common student setup)
- Air ports: **MagSafe charge** + **2× Thunderbolt/USB-C**
- Two external displays supported on current Air class (confirm on specs page if advising exotic configs)
- Monitors with **HDMI + DisplayPort only** (no USB-C):
  - Cheapest clean: `USB-C→HDMI` + `USB-C→DP` (4K60 class), charge via MagSafe
  - Optional single dock with HDMI+DP+PD later
- If user already has **large monitors + iPad Sidecar**, default **13"** not 15"; keep base **16GB/512GB** unless workload proven.

## TRX / in-store script (no CC)
1. Try 13" vs 15" physically if undecided.
2. Ask: Education verify in-store? Gift-card promo still active?
3. Ask: split cash + debit OK?
4. Do **not** promise Apple 0% without CC / on Edu order.
5. Confirm AppleCare+ price on the bag before paying.
