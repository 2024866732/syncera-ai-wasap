This Privacy Policy explains how Darksea Network Sdn Bhd ("Company") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the SYNCERA Software ("Software") and related services (darksea.network).

## 1. Summary (Plain Language)

- **We do NOT collect your business data.** All conversations, contacts, and KB entries stay on your computer.
- We only collect what's necessary to provide the Software (your email at purchase, license activations, optional crash reports).
- We never sell your data.
- You control optional features (Drive backup, cloud AI) that may transmit data externally.
- You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data we hold.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you purchase a license or contact us:
- **Name** (for invoice / receipt)
- **Email address**
- **Phone number** (optional, for support)
- **Business name** (optional, for invoice)
- **Malaysian IC / SSM business registration number** (optional, for SST tax invoice)
- **Billing address** (for tax purposes if applicable)
- **Payment method information** — processed by payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, bank); we do NOT store full card numbers

### 2.2 Information Collected by Software (Limited)

The SYNCERA Software is designed to be privacy-first. It does NOT phone home to us by default. The following limited information may be collected only if you opt in:

- **Anonymous crash reports** (opt-in) — stack trace, OS version, app version. No business data included.
- **Anonymous usage telemetry** (opt-in, planned for v1.5) — feature usage frequency, error rates. No identifying or content data.

### 2.3 Information NOT Collected

We do NOT collect:
- Your customer conversations
- Your contacts list
- Your business knowledge base content
- Your AI personas or prompts
- Your message content (in or out)
- Your WhatsApp authentication keys
- Your Google Drive data
- Any media files (images, videos, documents)

These all reside exclusively on your device.

### 2.4 Third-Party Data Flows (User-Controlled)

If you enable optional features, data may flow to third parties:

| Feature | Data Sent | Recipient | Your Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp connection | Messages (E2E encrypted) | WhatsApp / Meta | Required if using WA features |
| Pollinations.ai backend | AI prompt + KB context | Pollinations.ai | Choose another backend |
| Ollama / LM Studio (local) | AI prompt | localhost only | N/A — stays on your PC |
| Google Drive Backup | Database file | Your Google Drive | Enable / disable in Settings |
| DuckDuckGo AI search | Search query | DuckDuckGo | Disable in Settings |

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Our Security Practices
- Encrypted backups of customer database
- Limited employee access (need-to-know)
- Strong password policy + 2FA on critical accounts
- Regular security reviews

### 6.2 Software Security
- Renderer process sandboxed (no direct file/network access)
- IPC bridge with strict whitelisting
- Dependency auditing via `npm audit`
- Optional code signing (EV certificate) — roadmap

### 6.3 No System is 100% Secure
While we implement industry-standard security, no method of transmission or storage is fully secure. Use the Software at your own risk and maintain your own backups.

## 7. Your Rights

Under PDPA (Malaysia) and GDPR (EU), you have the following rights:

### 7.1 Right of Access
You may request a copy of personal data we hold about you. We will respond within 21 days (PDPA) or 30 days (GDPR).

### 7.2 Right of Correction
If your data is inaccurate, you may request correction.

### 7.3 Right of Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")
You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to:
- Legal obligation to retain (e.g. tax records)
- Active license — deletion may invalidate your license

### 7.4 Right to Data Portability (GDPR)
You may request your data in a portable format (JSON / CSV).

### 7.5 Right to Object
You may object to processing for marketing (use unsubscribe link in emails).

### 7.6 Right to Withdraw Consent
You may withdraw consent at any time for opt-in features.

### 7.7 Right to Complain
You may lodge a complaint with:
- **Malaysia:** Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP) — https://www.pdp.gov.my
- **EU:** Your national Data Protection Authority

### 7.8 Exercising Your Rights
Email **contact@example.com** with subject `[PRIVACY] Data Request — <your request type>`. Include:
- Your full name
- Email used at purchase
- License key (if applicable)
- Specific request

We will verify your identity before processing.
