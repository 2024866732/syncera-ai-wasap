## Pricing FAQ

### Why is it so much cheaper than competitors?
Competitors charge RM200-2000/month subscriptions because they host your data on their servers (high recurring infrastructure cost). SYNCERA runs on YOUR PC — we have zero infrastructure cost to recover. One-time price reflects software licensing only.

### Is RM999 a yearly fee?
No. **One-time.** Pay once, own forever. No subscriptions.

### What about future updates?
- Patches (1.0.1, 1.0.2): FREE forever
- Minor versions (1.1, 1.2): FREE for v1.x license holders
- Major versions (2.0+): 50% upgrade discount for existing customers

### Can I get a free trial?
We offer a **demo session** instead — 30-minute live walkthrough via Zoom. No trial version exists due to:
- Software has zero ongoing cost for us to support trials
- Hard to enforce 7-day deactivation without DRM (v1.0 has no DRM)
- 7-day money-back guarantee = effective trial

### What if I want to cancel my license?
Licenses are perpetual — no cancellation needed. You simply uninstall.

### Do you offer student/NGO discounts?
Yes — 30% off Personal License for verified students or registered NGOs in Malaysia. Email proof of status.
