HAFJET — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

1. About Us

HAFJET (Hafizi Gadget M Sdn Bhd) operates an automated WhatsApp chatbot to provide customer support for our mobile phone repair services in Malaysia.

2. Information We Collect

When you message our WhatsApp bot, we receive and process:

  • WhatsApp phone number — to identify conversation and send replies
  • Display name — as shown on your WhatsApp profile
  • Message content — the text you send to our bot
  • Timestamps — date and time of each message

We do not collect photos, videos, location, contacts, or any media shared on WhatsApp — our bot only processes text messages.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Answering inquiries about repair pricing and services
  • Providing repair job status updates
  • Sharing store information (hours, location, contact)
  • Improving bot response accuracy

4. Data Retention

Message data is retained for a maximum of 30 days for operational and debugging purposes. After 30 days, all message content is automatically deleted from our systems. Phone numbers used for active conversations are retained only for the duration of the ongoing support session.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties.

Your messages are processed through Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp) as the messaging platform provider. Meta's own privacy policy applies to data transmitted through their services. We do not share data with any other external parties.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit and at rest, including HTTPS encryption for all communications and access controls on our servers hosted on Microsoft Azure.

7. Your Rights

Under Malaysian data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us directly
  • Opt out at any time by ceasing to interact with the bot
  • Inquire about what data we hold about you

To exercise any of these rights, send a WhatsApp message to +60 11-4956 1698 or email us.

8. Compliance

This service complies with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia and the WhatsApp Business Terms of Service.
